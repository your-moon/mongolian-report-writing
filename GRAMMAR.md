# Grammar Reference — Cases, Vowel Harmony, Evidentiality

Sourced from the 2018 official Cyrillic orthography dictionary (mn.wikipedia.org reproduction of its rule text, §1–35), the Тийн ялгалын ай case table, and real examples pulled from ~85 news/legal/government articles across ikon.mn, montsame.mn, news.mn, gogo.mn, legalinfo.mn, and others.

## 1. The 8 noun cases (Тийн ялгал)

Mongolian marks grammatical role by suffix, not word order. The suffix vowel is chosen by vowel harmony (§2) and the stem's final-sound class.

Two rarer suffixes exist (тодотгохын -н "attributive"; хэмжихийн -цаа/-цээ/-цоо/-цөө "extent") — uncommon in formal prose, flagged for completeness only.

Which variant attaches depends on vowel harmony (below) **plus** the stem's final-consonant class (2018 rule §14–18): dative is **-д** after a vowel or м/н/г/л/б/в/р, but **-т** after д/т/ж/з/с/ш/ц/ч/х.

## 2. Vowel harmony (эгшгийн зохицол)

This is a **codified rule**, not a stylistic tendency — §7 of the 2018 orthography dictionary: *"Монгол хэлний нэг үгэнд эр, эм эгшиг хамтран орохгүй"* (a masculine and a feminine vowel do not co-occur within one native word).

Three vowel classes (§6–8):
- **Эр эгшиг (masculine):** а, о, у, я, ё, ю(у), ы
- **Эм эгшиг (feminine):** э, ө, ү, е, ю(ү)
- **Саармаг эгшиг (neutral):** и, й — combine with either, default to feminine suffix behavior (ишиг→ишгээр, not ишгаар)

§8 harmony table (first-syllable vowel → suffix vowel): а/у→а; э/ү/и→э; о→о; ө→ө.

Real minimal-pair confirmation from the corpus: **"8.6 тэрбум ам.доллароор"** (о-stem → -оор) vs **"2 жилийн хугацаагаар"** (а-stem → -аар) — not interchangeable. Ablative shown side by side: "өдрөөс эхлэн" (-өөс, о-class) vs "76 даргаас" (-ас, а-class).

**ы vs ий (§9):** long ы only in masculine words (ардын, нарны, хотыг); ий only in feminine words (хүний, эрдмийг, өвлийн).

**Practical rule: check the word's own first-syllable vowel before choosing a suffix — not the previous word in the sentence, not an assumed default.** That's where careless writing (including translated/generated Mongolian) most often slips.

**§27–29 (vowel-dropping, гээгдэх эгшиг):** a stem vowel is sometimes correctly dropped when a suffix attaches (олон→олноос), but a **proper noun keeps its vowel**: Матад→Матадаас, NOT Матдаас (§27, exception 1). Don't over-apply vowel-dropping to names.

## 3. Verb tense / aspect / evidentiality — the section that matters most for reports

| Ending | Function | Real example | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **-сан/-сэн/-сон/-сөн** | Completed action stated as settled fact, neutral evidentiality. **The workhorse of formal writing.** | "сүүлийн 5 жилд хэрэгжүүлсэн"; "З.Энхболд буруутай нь нотлогдоогүй тул цагаатсан." | montsame.mn / ikon.mn |
| **-жээ/-чээ** | Reportative/inferential — "it turns out that," "it is reported that." Marks the writer relaying **someone else's** finding, not direct witness. | "...баривчлагдсан тухай ТАСС агентлаг өчигдөр мэдээлжээ." (explicit source cited — clearest hearsay marker) | ikon.mn |
| **-лаа/-лээ/-лоо/-лөө** | Recent past — the speaker/institution's **own** immediate completed action, "just now, first-hand." | "...УИХ-ын дарга Д.Амарбаясгаланд өргөн барилаа." (the ministry's own press office reporting its own minister's act) | montsame.mn |
| **-даг/-дэг/-дог/-дөг** | Habitual/generic truth, not a one-off event. Rare in hard news; appears in procedural/legal explanatory text. | "Улсын дээд шүүхийн тогтоол ... нийтлэгддэг." | legalinfo.mn |
| **-на/-нэ/-но/-нө** | Future / scheduled / general fact. Dominant in laws, resolutions, weather bulletins. | "Хууль батлагдсан өдрөөсөө хэрэгжинэ." | ikon.mn |

### Report-register guidance (synthesized from the corpus pattern — flagged as synthesis, not a directly-quoted rule)

- Default to **-сан/-сэн** for facts the report itself is asserting or has verified.
- Use **-жээ** / **-сан байна** **only** when explicitly attributing a finding to a cited external source ("...гэж мэдээлжээ", "...тогтоожээ").
- Use **-лаа/-лээ/-в** sparingly — only for the report-issuing body's own immediate action.
- Use **-на/-нэ** for stated future obligations, scheduled effect dates, or procedures.
- **Never let -жээ leak into the report's own direct claim** — it makes an asserted fact sound secondhand and undercuts the report's authority.

## 4. Common mistakes

- **Vowel-harmony slips** — picking a suffix that "sounds closest to English" or copying the previous word's suffix instead of checking the current stem's own vowel class (see §2).
- **Comma-splice run-ons** — legislative titling chains 4+ genitive phrases into one subject-verb frame (a real, cited genre pattern in mof.gov.mn bill announcements) — don't imitate this in general report writing; break into a list or shorter sentences.
- **Calquing English structure word-for-word** — no real bad example was found in the corpus (professionally-edited Mongolian avoids it), so this is a reasoned, explicitly-labeled constructed pair:
  - ❌ Calqued: *"Судалгаагаар X нь Y-г үүсгэсэн болохыг олж мэдэв"* (mechanically translating "The study found that X caused Y")
  - ✅ Natural: *"Судалгаагаар X нь Y-д хүргэдэг болох нь тогтоогдсон"* / *"X нь Y-г үүсгэдгийг судалгаагаар тогтоов"* — Mongolian prefers the established-fact verb (тогтоогдсон/тогтоов) at the very end, governing the whole preceding clause, not a translated "that"-clause nested mid-sentence.
- **Inconsistent loanword/technical-term script** — needless foreign-word substitution for an existing Mongolian word is a named, catalogued style fault (nairuulga.mn documents this with an error-rate study finding 45.5–64.9% of sampled newspaper text affected). Prefer the native/established term unless the loanword is the actual term of art.
- **Genitive misattribution** — "Ерөнхийлөгчийн сонгууль" ("President's election") is flagged as misleading via genitive -ийн, since it implies the president runs the election rather than being its object. Genitive-chaining nouns can assert an unintended possessor/agent/object relationship — check what -ын/-ийн is actually claiming.

## 5. Punctuation

- **Цэг (period):** ends declarative/imperative sentences; after given-name-initial abbreviations (Л.Оюун-Эрдэнэ — appears in nearly every formal article); separates numeric date components (2025.02.26); follows numbered list items.
- **Давхар цэг (colon):** introduces explanations, lists, direct quotations, ratios/times (10:4, 12:00 цагт).
- **Цуваа цэг (ellipsis):** marks an incomplete/trailed-off sentence or omitted quotation portion.
- **Quotation marks:** corpus consistently uses straight " ", not « » — zero instances of « » found across the fetched corpus. Treat as observed practice (older print Mongolian sometimes uses «»), not a confirmed hard rule.
- **Comma before a fronted subordinate/adverbial clause:** consistently used in real sentences, e.g. "Зарлиг гаргасантай холбогдуулан өнөөдөр ... Ерөнхийлөгч шүүгч нарт..." — comma after the fronted "-тай холбогдуулан" clause.
- **Never break a syllable across two lines** (legalinfo.mn rule).
- **Lists/enumeration in legal/government text** comma-separates full titled noun phrases within one sentence rather than using bullets — genre-specific to legislative announcements, not general advice.

## 6. Numbers, dates, currency

- **Percentages:** digit + space + "хувь" is standard in careful running prose ("6.4 хувиар өссөн"); bare "%" also appears in some outlets/registers ("32%-иар"). **Pick one and hold it in a given document.** The case ending on хувь itself correctly varies by grammatical role: -аар (instrumental, "by X percent"), -ийн (genitive), -тай (comitative, "at X percent").
- **Percentage-point vs. percentage — keep distinct:** "2 нэгж хувиар нэмэгдүүлж, 12 хувьд хүргэх" — "нэгж хувь" (percentage point) vs. plain "хувь" (percent) in the same sentence. English "point" vs. "percent" often gets flattened in translation — don't let that happen.
- **Large figures:** "тэрбум" (billion) / "сая" (million) / "их наяд" (trillion) spelled as words after the digit, with the currency unit carrying its own case suffix: "8.6 тэрбум ам.доллароор экспортолсон".
- **Official/standard money format:** numeral + parenthetical spelled-out form — "3,603,000 (гурван сая зургаан зуун гурван мянга)" (MNS 5140-2:2021).
- **Dates — two coexisting formats:** spelled-out ordinal for running/legal text — "2026 оны 01 дүгээр сарын 01-ний өдрөөс"; numeric dot-separated for headers/bylines — "2007.03.06" / "2025.02.26". Prefer the spelled-out form in report body text for clarity and audit-friendliness.
- **Vote/statistical results:** raw count + derived percentage together, joined by "буюу" — "110 гишүүн оролцсоноос 90 гишүүн буюу 81.8 хувь нь ... баталлаа." This apposition pattern (count + буюу + percent) is the standard way Mongolian formal reporting presents such results.
